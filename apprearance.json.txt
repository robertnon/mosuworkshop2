Party B is a professional Apex Legends team from the Southeast Asia region, qualified to compete in the Apex Legends YEAR 6 series of events.

Whereas Party A intends to cooperate with Party B regarding the Apex Legends YEAR 6 Las Vegas (USA) Playoffs, the two parties, through equal negotiation, have reached the following Agreement, to be jointly observed by both parties.

Article 1 — Scope of Cooperation
The two parties shall cooperate in the Apex Legends YEAR 6 series of events. Party B's roster shall compete under the name designated by Party A in the Apex Legends YEAR 6 Las Vegas Playoffs and any subsequent qualifying events. Party A shall pay the cooperation fee and bear the agreed reimbursement expenses in accordance with this Agreement, and shall be entitled to a share of event prize money, team brand exposure, and other rights.
All competing players of Party B shall bear joint and several liability for the obligations under this Agreement. If any player breaches this Agreement, Party A has the right to require all competing players to jointly and severally bear liability for compensation.
Article 2 — Cooperation Period
The basic cooperation period is 3 months, from October 1, 2026 to December 31, 2026.
If Party B successfully advances to the Apex Legends YEAR 6 Japan Sapporo Championship, this Agreement shall automatically renew for 2 months, during which the rights and obligations of both parties shall be the same as during the basic cooperation period.
Article 3 — Cooperation Fee and Payment
During the cooperation period, Party A shall pay the cooperation fee to Party B's team players on a monthly basis, at the rate of RMB 6,000 per player per month.
If Party B successfully advances to the Apex Legends YEAR 6 Japan Sapporo Championship, Party A shall raise the cooperation fee for the 2-month renewal period to RMB 8,000 per player per month.
The cooperation fee shall be paid monthly. Party A shall pay the previous month's cooperation fee in full, before the 15th of each month, into the bank account(s) designated by Party B's team players. Article 4 — Distribution of Event Prize Money
Prize money officially awarded under this Agreement for the Las Vegas (USA) Playoffs and the Japan Sapporo Championship shall be paid by the event organizer, in full, to the club account designated by Party A.
Within 15 business days after Party A receives the full prize money and completes the relevant tax processing, the prize money shall be distributed in the proportion of [Party A 25%, Party B's competing player team 75%]. Party A has the right to withhold and pay, in accordance with law, the individual income tax and other statutory taxes/fees for which Party B is responsible, and shall pay the after-tax balance due to Party B into the account(s) designated by Party B. Prize money shall be calculated in the currency actually paid by the event organizer; if conversion into RMB is required, it shall be converted using the median rate published by the People's Bank of China on the day Party A actually receives the prize money. Cross-border remittance fees shall be jointly borne by both parties in proportion to the prize distribution ratio. If the prize money is not paid, or is delayed, due to reasons attributable to the event organizer, Party A shall not bear liability for breach of contract.
Article 5 — Change of Team Name and Identity
Both parties confirm that Party B's official competing team name and identity/branding must be changed to Titan Esports Club (abbreviated TEC) immediately on the day the official transfer/information-change window opens, following the conclusion of the Apex Legends YEAR 6 Southeast Asia regional finals.
Party B shall ensure that all competing players cooperate in fully completing the update of team information and player affiliation information within the official event system; Party A shall cooperate by providing relevant brand materials and support for the relevant filings/notifications.
Article 6 — Rights and Obligations of Both Parties
(1) Rights and Obligations of Party A
Pay the cooperation fee and prize money share to Party B in full and on time, in accordance with this Agreement.
During offline international events, provide meal expense reimbursement to Party B's competing players, under the following rules:
(1) Reimbursement standard: a cap of USD 50 per person per day, reimbursed based on actual expenditure;
(2) Reimbursement process: Party B's personnel shall first advance the meal costs themselves, retain valid dining receipts, and submit them together to Party A for review and reimbursement after the event has fully concluded;
(3) Where actual daily expenditure is below USD 50, reimbursement shall be based on the actual amount spent; where actual daily expenditure exceeds USD 50, reimbursement shall be capped at USD 50.
Apart from the meal expense reimbursement expressly agreed in this Agreement, airfare, transportation, accommodation, insurance, and other travel expenses of Party B's competing players and accompanying staff shall be borne entirely by Party B itself. Party A shall bear no such expenses.
Has the right to supervise and provide guidance regarding Party B's participation in events and brand exposure.
Party A has the right to use the images, competition footage, and materials of Party B's players during events for brand promotion, social media operations, and commercial cooperation. The licensing fee for image rights is already included in the cooperation fee paid by Party A, and Party A is not required to pay any additional fee. Party B may not use the TEC identity/branding for any activity outside the events covered by this Agreement without authorization.
(2) Rights and Obligations of Party B
Has the right to receive the cooperation fee and prize money share in accordance with this Agreement.
Shall be responsible, on its own, for the visa applications, processing, and all related costs of the competing players; if a visa cannot be obtained, is delayed, or other circumstances affecting participation arise, Party B shall arrange substitute personnel on its own to ensure normal participation, with the related costs and consequences borne by Party B. Substitute personnel must equally comply with all obligations under this Agreement — including confidentiality, brand exposure, and competition standards — and Party B shall ensure that substitute personnel fulfill all responsibilities under this Agreement.
Party B shall reserve, within its competing roster and accompanying delegation, [2] team guest seats, [2] team media seats, and [2] accompanying staff slots for Party A.
Strictly complete the official change of team name and identity in accordance with this Agreement, and cooperate with Party A's brand exposure requirements.
Guarantee that it possesses legitimate eligibility to compete, comply with the official event rules, and follow the event organizer's unified arrangements.
During the cooperation period, Party B may not change competing players without Party A's prior written consent; where a change is genuinely necessary due to special circumstances, Party B shall submit an application and the relevant information of the substitute player to Party A in advance, and the change may only be made after written confirmation from Party A.
Article 7 — Confidentiality
Both parties shall keep confidential the content of this Agreement, the cooperation fee, prize money standards, business cooperation information, competitive tactics/strategy, internal operational materials, and all other non-public information.
Without the other party's prior written consent, neither party may disclose the above confidential information to any third party, except where disclosure is required by law/regulation or compelled by a judicial authority.
This confidentiality obligation shall take effect from the date this Agreement is signed and shall remain in effect for 3 years after the Agreement terminates.
Article 8 — Liability for Breach and Mutual Restrictions
Any breach of this Agreement by either party shall be deemed a breach of contract, and the breaching party shall compensate the non-breaching party for all direct economic losses and loss of anticipated benefits arising therefrom, including but not limited to Party A's promotional expenses invested in this cooperation, material production costs, loss of business opportunities, loss of goodwill, and reasonable expenses incurred in enforcing its rights.
If Party A is late in paying compensation or prize money, it shall pay Party B liquidated damages at a rate of 0.05% of the outstanding amount for each day of delay.
If Party B fails to change the team name/identity as agreed, fails to resolve visa and substitute-player issues on its own, discloses confidential information without authorization, violates event rules resulting in disqualification, or if Party B or its players engage in match-fixing, deliberate loss of matches, or make inappropriate public statements that damage Party A's brand reputation and commercial interests, Party A has the right to unilaterally terminate this Agreement and require Party B to compensate Party A for all losses (including but not limited to litigation costs, attorney's fees, and costs of preservation/injunctive measures).
Article 9 — Other Provisions
The Chinese and English versions of this Agreement have equal legal effect, and reflect the consistent intent of both parties.
Any dispute arising from this Agreement shall first be resolved through negotiation between the parties; if negotiation fails, either party may file suit with the people's court having jurisdiction over Party A's registered address.
This Agreement is executed in two counterparts, with Party A and Party B each holding one copy. It shall take effect on the date both parties sign and affix their seals/signatures.
A scanned copy of this Agreement has the same legal effect as the original.
